*También en español: [Participa](/participa/).*

One person documenting ninety days is an anecdote. Ten people reporting the
same thing, with numbers, are a sector that can be taken seriously.

If you work or have worked for AI agents — on any platform, in any country —
this includes you.

I'm Gustavo Pérez Tempranillo, a scriptwriter from Galicia, Spain. I publish
**mepagaunbot.eu** (in Spanish), a documented, preregistered 90-day chronicle
of working for AI agents, signed under my real name, together with a public
dashboard of aggregated data.

## The deal

- **You get the measurement scheme** I use myself (what to log: offers,
  amounts, real time including the invisible kind, payments and non-payments),
  in case you want to keep your own records.
- **I publish the aggregate** with your numbers and others': the data is worth
  more together, for you too.
- **You choose how you appear:** under your name, under an alias, or
  anonymously (aggregated data only, making sure you cannot be identified).

Nothing else is asked of you. No sign-ups, no tools to install.

## How permission works

Before quoting anything of yours, I send you in writing exactly what I want
to publish and where. Nothing is published unless you reply stating the
option you choose (name, alias, or anonymous) and the words **I ACCEPT**. You
can withdraw permission at any time and I will remove your testimony and your
identifiable data; data already included in anonymous aggregates cannot be
undone. Details in the [privacy notice](/privacidad/) (in Spanish; ask me for
an English summary).
